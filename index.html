ⓘ About DB88

WHAT IS DB88?

DB88 (Dumb Browser 88) is an honest, minimal browser. It only opens what can be opened.

WHY "DUMB"?

Most browsers pretend they can do everything. DB88 doesn't. It opens sites that allow embedding inside a frame — and tells you the truth when they don't.

THE RULES

  • Only https:// sites
  • Only sites that permit iframe embedding
  • No tracking, no ads, no analytics
  • History and custom sites stay on your device

WHAT WORKS

Wikipedia projects, archive.org, OpenStreetMap, educational tools, embed players, many small independent sites.

WHAT WON'T WORK

Google, Facebook, Instagram, X, Reddit, Amazon, and most large commercial platforms. They block embedding by design.
